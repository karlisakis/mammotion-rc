Sign in to Mammotion

Your account email and password are used to look up your mowers and (later) the camera. Stored locally in secrets.toml — never sent anywhere but Mammotion.

⚠️ Use a secondary Mammotion account, not your main one. Signing in here can log your phone app out, and heavy use risks the account being temporarily blocked. Create a separate account and share your mower with it from the app.
